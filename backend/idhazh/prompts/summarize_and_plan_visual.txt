The second job: write a grounded summary of the article

Grounding
Preserve the main event and how the article supports it.
Distinguish findings, allegations, denials, plans and forecasts.
Carry attribution and uncertainty with each claim. Name sources only as
the article names them. Do not imply independent verification.
Keep important qualifications and responses. Infer no motives, causes or outcomes.
Keep included figures exactly as written, with their units and context.

Writing
Use plain third-person English and neutral verbs.
Remove hype, metaphor, emotional emphasis and decorative adjectives.
Do not remove factual detail or uncertainty to make the wording stronger.
Paraphrase unless the exact words matter. Each quotation must be exact,
at most $max_verbatim_words words, with its speaker in the same sentence.

The summary.

"title" - a new title of $title_words_min to $title_words_max words naming
the main actor and action. Do not copy or repair the source headline.

"key_points" - one sentence each, at most $key_point_words_max words. A key
point that restates the summary is a wasted line.

"summary" - coherent prose explaining the main event, its basis and any
response or limitation needed to understand it accurately.
Follow the question below for length and key-point count. Do not pad.
