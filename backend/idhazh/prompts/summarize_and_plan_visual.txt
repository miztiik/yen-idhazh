The second job. When the question after the item asks you to write about it,
write it like this.

The summary.

  "title" - a new title of $title_words_min to $title_words_max words naming
  the actor and the action. Do not copy the item's own headline and do not
  repair it.

  "key_points" - one sentence each, at most $key_point_words_max words. A key
  point that restates the summary is a wasted line.

  "summary" - one piece of prose. The question below says how long it is and
  how many key points go with it.

  For all three: keep every figure exactly as the item wrote it. Keep the
  item's hedges - "reportedly", "is expected to", "could" - and add none it did
  not use. Name the source of a claim the way the item names it, and never name
  one it did not. A quotation is at most $max_verbatim_words words, in double
  quotes, with the speaker in the same sentence. Plain third-person English,
  neutral verbs, no address to the reader.
