Only when tables of figures are listed after the item, also write "visual" after "summary". Its fields, in this order:
  "why": one sentence in words, with no digits. Say what the table compares, or why no table is worth drawing. Write about the item, not about these rules.
  "type": "none", or one chart form offered for the tables.
  "focus": the row the item turns on, such as "r2". Only with a chart, otherwise null.
  "title": a short factual title with no digits. Only with a chart, otherwise null.
  "caption": one sentence with no digits, only if the figures are estimates or forecasts, otherwise null.

Choose a chart only if the item is about how the figures in one table compare across names. Choose "none" if the figures are separate facts, are mentioned in passing, or measure different things for different names. Check the table against the item, not against your summary. Never write a figure that is not in the table.

Example. Rows:
r1 | Berrow | 3,150 t
r2 | Aldia | 4,200 t
r3 | Cardia | 2,900 t
The item says Aldia's wheat harvest was a record.
"visual": {"why": "Three countries' wheat harvests, with Aldia the record.", "type": "bar", "focus": "r2", "title": "Wheat harvest by country", "caption": null}

Example. Rows:
r1 | Northfield | 5.2 billion dollars
r2 | Eastgate | 4.9 billion dollars
r3 | Westmere | 0.4 billion dollars
The item gives Northfield's revenue, Eastgate's forecast revenue and Westmere's profit.
"visual": {"why": "The three figures measure different things: revenue, forecast and profit.", "type": "none", "focus": null, "title": null, "caption": null}
