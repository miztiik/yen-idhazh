When requested, finish "summary" before "visual".

Choose "visual" only when a supported numeric comparison or relationship is
quicker to grasp than prose. Reusing summary facts is allowed; numeric density
alone is insufficient. Otherwise choose "none".

For "none": set "purpose", "type", "declared_whole_element_id", "title" and
"caption" to null; leave every list and encoding channel empty; explain in "why".

For "visual":
  "purpose": the reader's task, chosen before "type".
  "type": an offered form satisfying the generated guide.
  "encodings": source IDs, aligned by displayed observation. Include every
  channel; unused channels are empty. Within each measured channel, compare
  compatible measures, definitions, populations and denominators with
  convertible units. For scatter/bubble, each point's measures must share one
  subject; points must have distinct subjects. Pair dates with values only
  where the article establishes the association; never resolve relative dates.
  "declared_whole_element_id": null except for pie/stacked_bar. Cite an
  independently stated whole, never a part or calculated total. Parts must
  exhaust that same whole. For stacked_bar, category cites the whole and series
  names parts; for pie, category names parts.
  "labels": source IDs whose own words name marks and axes.
  "annotations": source references resolving to exactly one displayed numeric
  measurement, including through its source inputs.
  "why": a short reason for this comparison and form.
  "title": a short factual title.
  "caption": essential scope, attribution or qualification; null if unnecessary.
  Keep estimates, forecasts and population/denominator distinctions visible.
  Choose "none" if necessary distinctions cannot be preserved.

"confidence": your self-rating from 0 to 1, not source uncertainty.

Use only finalized-table IDs. Any number in title, caption or why must be
supported by the selected chart references; do not add unplotted numeric facts.
Check meaning against the original article, not just the summary; earlier
labels are judgments, not proof. Do not supply plotted values, dates or layout
settings; code resolves measurements from the selected IDs. Source names may
retain their written digits.
