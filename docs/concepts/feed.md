# Earned Feeds

**Last Updated**: 2026-09-09

**A feed earns its place.** Being listed in the source catalog is not an
entitlement to requests, model work, reader space or permanent membership.
Consistent, useful delivery over time earns those opportunities. F.E.E.D aims
to improve the reader's coverage within the available resources, not to
maximize article count or make its own score rise.

## Intelligent F.E.E.D - I Feed

F.E.E.D expands to Feed Evaluation & Execution Decider. The proposed system
connects observations, weighted assessments, age-weighted reputation, selection
and recovery in one feedback loop. Its methods and parameters are adjustable
guidelines, not a fixed rule set.

The complete design, equations, contracts and rationale live in
[Intelligent F.E.E.D - I Feed](../architecture/sources/i-feed.md). This concept
page does not duplicate them. The design is not implemented; current operational
behavior remains in [source health](../architecture/sources/health.md).
