# Plan 65.2 - Summary fidelity and drift judges, delivery version

**Last Updated**: 2026-10-23
**Level**: 5 (each row states its own level; Table A, A6 lists the Level 5 rows)

## North Star

The maintainer's intent of 2026-10-12 (plan 65, Table Q, Q58 and Q64). Every row, research brief and review is checked against these lines; a proposal that breaks one names the line and why.

- **Unsupervised improvement of summary quality.** Measure, test, tune settings and move to newer models with no person in the loop; nothing is trained (Q73, E3); every automatic step is bounded, recorded and has an off switch.
- **Quality over brevity.** A summary is as long as its content needs; length is recorded, never targeted or penalised.
- **Built for tomorrow.** 10,000 summaries a day, longer summaries and new models; nothing is sized on today's numbers.
- **Sample, never check everything.** Approved second models, from a different family than the summarizer, check a small sample of summaries against the article text the summarizer was given; sample sizes follow from the precision wanted, and the digest's work step does no extra work.
- **Config-driven and evolvable.** Every number is in config; judges, scorers, metrics and ledgers have a lifecycle (active, paused, retired); records are enrolled ledgers in state/raw and state/compact, and kept article text is a small rolling sample deleted on schedule.
- **Metrics, not messages.** Stored metrics and code decide - no GitHub issues and no heuristics on wording; the other autotune judges (feed quality, feed reliability, content similarity) consume this plan's metrics to tune themselves.
