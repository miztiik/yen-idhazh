The rule (plan 65, Section 4; full form in the review record, section SP5):

- **Expected value.** For summary $i$ and judged member $m$, $y_{mi}$ is the member's value. The expected value is the baseline mean of the summary's length band $b_i$ plus a source offset shrunk toward zero by $k$ = source_shrinkage_rows, and the residual is what is left:
    $$e_{mi} = \mu_m(b_i) + u_m(s_i), \qquad u_m(s) = \frac{\sum_{i \in B,\ s_i = s} \left( y_{mi} - \mu_m(b_i) \right)}{n_B(s) + k}, \qquad r_{mi} = y_{mi} - e_{mi}$$
    Watched members are never adjusted: $r_{mi} = y_{mi}$.
- **Daily value.** $x_{md}$ is the mean residual of day $d$ (the median of $y$ for length_median), and $n_d$ is the day's row count; a day with fewer than min_day_rows rows is thin and left out.
- **Windows.** The recent window $R$ is the recent_days days ending on the judged night $t$; the baseline $B$ is the baseline_days days ending baseline_lag_days days before $t$. Each holds only its valid days, on the member's current instrument: a day with no run, a failed run or a thin day is skipped, never filled in. The member is tested only when $R$ holds at least min_recent_days valid days and $B$ at least min_baseline_days; otherwise its row is stored as too_few_days.
- **Test statistic.** Recent days carry recency weights $a_d = \lambda (1 - \lambda)^{t - d}$, with $\lambda$ = recency_lambda; baseline days are weighted by row count alone; $h_m$ is $+1$ or $-1$ by harm direction:
    $$T_m = h_m \left( \frac{\sum_{d \in R} a_d n_d x_{md}}{\sum_{d \in R} a_d n_d} - \frac{\sum_{d \in B} n_d x_{md}}{\sum_{d \in B} n_d} \right)$$
- **No-change case.** $N$ = reshuffles deals $\pi$ of the whole days of $R \cup B$ into the two windows, one deal set for every member, give $T^{\ast}_{m,\pi}$. The member's score is standardised against the deals and widened for its day-to-day correlation $\rho_m$ (rho); $z^{\ast}_{m,\pi}$ is each deal standardised the same way, unwidened:
    $$z_m = \frac{T_m - \operatorname{mean}_{\pi} T^{\ast}_{m,\pi}}{\operatorname{sd}_{\pi} T^{\ast}_{m,\pi}} \bigg/ \sqrt{\frac{1 + \rho_m}{1 - \rho_m}}, \qquad p_m = \frac{1 + \operatorname{count} \lbrace \pi : z^{\ast}_{m,\pi} \ge z_m \rbrace}{N + 1}$$
- **Decision.** One false-discovery family $F$ per (run_id, date): the active members. Benjamini-Hochberg at $q$ = false_discovery_rate sorts the p-values and crosses the $k$ smallest, where $k = \max \lbrace i : p_{(i)} \le i q / |F| \rbrace$. Paused members keep a p-value and get no q-value.
- **Drift reading.** An active judged member crossed on drift_consecutive_nights consecutive nights reads as drift; any other crossing is stored only. The reading is derived from stored rows by whatever reads them; nothing notifies anyone and no GitHub issue is used. Every row stores its effect raw and adjusted, its q-value, and the recorded inputs that changed inside its windows.
- **Changes.** A new model, prompt, quantization or runtime build is named on the rows and restarts nothing; a move in a member's own instrument stamp restarts that member (row #42).
