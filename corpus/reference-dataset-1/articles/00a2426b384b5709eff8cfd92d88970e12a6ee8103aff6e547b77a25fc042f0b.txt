Tensions escalated outside the Parliament Street Police Station in New Delhi as workers from the Indian Youth Congress and the Caucasus Janata Party staged protests demanding stringent legal action against Swatantra Bharatwaj. The unrest follows an alleged murderous assault on the father of activist Nishu Azad, who has sought legal support from Rahul Gandhi's team. Protesters and leaders, including Saurabh Das and Ashutosh Ranka, sought meetings with senior Delhi Police officials to demand the addition of attempt-to-murder sections in the First Information Report. Police authorities maintained that the medical legal certificate classified the injuries sustained by the victim, Sanjay Azad, as non-serious, leading to the omission of Section 109 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. Security forces, including central paramilitary personnel, were heavily deployed outside the police station as demonstrators continued their dharna, seeking immediate custody of the accused.
Nishu Azad, Chandra Shekhar Aazad & Leaders Reach Parliament Street Police Station Over Assault Case
High drama unfolded outside the Parliament Street Police Station in New Delhi as Nishu Azad, along with her father Sanjay Azad, Bhim Army chief Chandra Shekhar Aazad, and other leaders, arrived to meet senior police officials regarding the alleged assault on Sanjay Azad. Protesters and party workers gathered outside the barricaded police station, mounting pressure on the authorities and disrupting traffic along the street. A five-member delegation was permitted entry to present their demands to the Additional DCP, seeking the addition of stricter non-bailable sections, including attempt to murder, to the First Information Report. Delhi Police had initially invoked bailable sections based on the Medico-Legal Certificate from RML Hospital, which indicated non-serious laceration injuries. Tensions escalated following a video confession by the accused, who claimed responsibility for the assault and alleged political backing. The police are continuing their investigation to determine whether further stringent penal sections should be incorporated into the charge sheet.

An IndiGo aircraft operating flight 6E225 from Navi Mumbai to Srinagar suffered a minor collision with a pole while positioning at Bay Number 6 at Srinagar Airport. The incident occurred around 9:00 AM during the parking process when the aircraft came into contact with a pole. All passengers and crew members on board are safe, with no injuries reported. Passengers deplaned safely using stairs, and flight operations at Srinagar Airport continue normally without disruption. Authorities, including IndiGo and the Srinagar Airport Authority, have launched an investigation to determine whether a technical glitch or pilot error caused the incident.
A major controversy erupted in Kerala following remarks by Islamic scholar and Samastha leader Kanthapuram A.P. Aboobacker Musliyar regarding the role of women in society. Musliyar issued a clarification after facing severe criticism, stating that his organisation never advocated confining women or treating them as second-class citizens, noting that institutions under their management offer education to women. Earlier, Musliyar had asserted that women should primarily stay at home to preserve their dignity, as according to him, it was prescribed in the Holy Quran. Kerala Chief Minister V.D. Satheesan strongly countered this perspective, asserting that women must participate across all spheres of life and citing Islamic history where women led armies, ruled kingdoms, and managed major businesses. The political controversy follows a Samastha circular urging curbs on women participating publicly in festival celebrations, triggering widespread debate on constitutional democracy, gender equality, and progressive values in the state.
A senior priest of the Mar Thoma Church in Kerala has drawn criticism after suggesting that pre-marriage HIV tests should be made mandatory before wedding ceremonies conducted by the church. The priest cited recent campus testing initiatives undertaken by the Karnataka government following an increase in cases. Additional remarks advising parents regarding their daughters before marriage sparked widespread debate on social media. While some viewed premarital screening as a preventive health measure if applied equally to both brides and grooms, questions were raised concerning the specific focus on women.
