Death toll from Northern Cyprus ferry accident rises to 13
- 15 people remain missing after Filo Jet passenger vessel capsized off Girne last Sunday while traveling to Tasucu, Türkiye
Gizem Nisa Çebi Demir
05 September 2026•Update: 05 September 2026
ISTANBUL
The death toll from a ferry accident off Girne (Kyrenia) in the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC) has risen to 13, while 15 people remain missing, Prime Minister Unal Ustel said Saturday.
“With the recovery of the latest body, the number of our people who lost their lives in the accident has risen to 13, while the number of our missing people is now 15,” Ustel said.
Search efforts for those still missing are continuing following the accident involving the Filo Jet passenger vessel, which capsized off Girne while traveling from Girne Port to Tasucu in Mersin, Türkiye.
The vessel was carrying 267 people when it sank after departing Girne Kyrenia last Sunday, Aug. 30.
