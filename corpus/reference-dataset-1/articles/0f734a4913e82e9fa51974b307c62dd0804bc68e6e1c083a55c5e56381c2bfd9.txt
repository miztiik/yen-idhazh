Chinese battery technology is playing a growing role in Mexico’s renewable energy expansion as new solar and wind projects are required to include energy storage.
Chinese companies showcased storage systems and battery technologies at a major renewable energy expo in Mexico City.
But industry representatives warn that Mexico’s tariffs on Chinese imports could raise costs and slow deployment as the government considers additional trade restrictions.
CGTN’s Alasdair Baverstock reports from Mexico City.
