HYDERABAD: Australia’s Monash University has invited Singareni Collieries Company Limited (SCCL) to explore a partnership in the critical minerals sector, citing the country’s rich reserves and growing global demand for such resources.
A Monash delegation met SCCL chairman and managing director Dr Buddaprakash Jyoti at Singareni Bhavan on Monday evening and briefed him on opportunities in exploration, extraction, processing and refining of critical minerals. The university said it can offer technical expertise in advanced, environmentally sustainable mining practices and help turn research into commercially viable technologies, according to a media release.
The delegates stressed the need to move beyond raw mineral extraction and create additional value through processing and refining. They suggested that SCCL could leverage Monash University’s strengths in sustainable mining, mine closure planning, advanced technologies and skill development.
SCCL officials presented the company’s plans to enter the critical minerals space and noted that it has recently begun exploring gold and copper deposits in Karnataka. Dr Buddaprakash said SCCL is examining global opportunities in this sector and that possibilities for cooperation with Monash University in research, technology and skill development would be explored. He added that a high-level Singareni team will soon visit Australia to study the opportunities. Monash University Professor Yiannis Ventikos, Dean (Engineering) and other members took part in talks.
