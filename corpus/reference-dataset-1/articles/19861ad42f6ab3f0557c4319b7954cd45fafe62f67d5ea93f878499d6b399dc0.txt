Editing videos is one of those things most of us don’t have to do too often—at least not at an especially high level—so it’s understandable if you don’t have a dedicated professional-grade video editor. The built-in editor within your phone’s photo app works well enough for quickly cutting a video down to size.
But what if you want to combine multiple videos into a single file? Or do things like add captions or background music? That’s where timeline-based editing software comes in. These applications let you combine multiple video files into a single timeline, then tweak the transitions.
Finding a free, powerful video editing tool that works on any kind of device (and is actually easy to use!) has traditionally been a real challenge. Today, though, I’ve got the long-overdue universal answer.
