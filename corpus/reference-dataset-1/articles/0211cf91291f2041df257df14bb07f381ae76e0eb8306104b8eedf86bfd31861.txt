Earlier this year, we redesigned the Brickbuilder Partner Network Program for ISVs and Data Providers around a simple idea: partners that create more customer impact should earn deeper investment from Databricks. Today, we’re putting our money where our mouth is with the launch of our first Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum partner tiers.
As the saying goes, if you want to go fast, go alone; if you want to go far, go together. We intend to go far, and aim to reward the partners that help us get there the fastest.
The program has four tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum), and a partner's placement is earned across five pillars:
Tiers are anchored by the Partner Well-Architected Framework (PWAF), our prescriptive, AI-ready guidance for building secure, reliable integrations on Databricks. PWAF is how a partner demonstrates Technical Excellence: it's the bar for a validated integration, and it feeds directly into tier placement. Point your favorite AI-assisted tools at PWAF, build to the guidance, and you're building toward a higher tier.
When a customer is choosing between partner products on Databricks, tiers tell them the partner's solution has met a rigorous bar for architecture, security, and compliance, and has a track record of joint customer success. We hold that bar deliberately high, so a Silver, Gold, or Platinum partner is one that customers can build on with confidence. Less time vetting, more time building.
Like most things in life, our partner tiers give back more the more you put in. If you're a Databricks ISV partner or data provider, your tier is a roadmap. Every pillar comes with a clear set of requirements, and you can reference the resources linked in the Databricks Partner Portal to guide you through completing them. We've already seen partners commit to deeper investment specifically to reach the next tier, a sign the program is doing what we hoped: rewarding the partners who invest more with us. Partnership is a two-way street: the more you invest in shared customer success, the more visibility, co-selling, and go-to-market support you earn in return.
